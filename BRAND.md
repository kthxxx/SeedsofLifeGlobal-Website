# Seeds of Life Global Brand Guide

## Brand idea

**Planting seeds. Nurturing lives.**

Seeds of Life Global is a warm, faith-rooted community organization. Its identity should feel grounded, hopeful, and active: care that begins locally and grows outward through children, families, leaders, and shared work.

**Voice:** clear, gracious, practical, hopeful. Speak with warmth and specificity; favor invitations over claims. Use plain language around faith, purpose, learning, and community.

## Visual system

| Role | Name | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Forest | `#173B2A` |
| Supporting | Moss | `#58724D` |
| Accent | Clay | `#A75434` |
| Highlight | Sun | `#E8C957` |
| Canvas | Cream | `#F7F3E9` |
| Supporting canvas | Sand | `#EEE5D5` |

Use Forest as the anchoring color, Cream as the default canvas, Clay for small navigational or editorial accents, and Sun for moments of welcome, emphasis, and calls to action. Keep large areas calm; do not use Clay and Sun together for body text.

## Typography

- **Display:** Georgia / Times New Roman serif — editorial, human, and confident. Use for headlines, quotations, and key numbers.
- **Utility:** Arial / Helvetica sans-serif — simple, legible, and direct. Use for body copy, navigation, labels, and calls to action.
- Headlines use tight spacing and sentence case. Small labels use the sans-serif in uppercase with generous tracking.

## Image direction

Use candid, documentary-style images of people learning, serving, celebrating, and spending time together in Cebu. Favor natural daylight, tropical greens, warm skin tones, and real environments. Avoid staged corporate imagery, heavy filters, and photos that isolate children from their community context.

## Sample language

- **Headline:** Planting seeds. Nurturing lives.
- **Invitation:** Bring your care, skills, or partnership to the work.
- **Mission cue:** Growth takes root when people are given room to learn, care, and build together.
- **Sign-off:** Where purpose takes root and lives bear fruit.
